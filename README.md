# E-Commerce Customer Behavior Analysis
**Business Intelligence Project | MIS 473**

**Status:** In Progress 🚧

## Project Overview
A Business Intelligence project focused on analyzing e-commerce customer behavior, sales performance, and customer service efficiency using Power BI. The project aims to uncover behavioral patterns and factors associated with customer churn to support data-driven business decisions.

## Project Objectives
Apply practical Business Intelligence and data analytics techniques through:
- Assessing data quality and identifying data issues.
- Cleaning, preprocessing, and transforming raw data.
- Exploring customer behavior and identifying patterns and relationships.
- Defining and calculating relevant business KPIs.
- Developing interactive Power BI dashboards.
- Applying regression analysis and interpreting results.
- Translating findings into actionable business recommendations.

## Dataset
[E-Commerce Customer Behavior Dataset – Kaggle](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/dhairyajeetsingh/ecommerce-customer-behavior-dataset)

- **Records:** 50,000
- **Attributes:** 25
- **Coverage:** 8 countries and 40 cities
- **Format:** CSV
